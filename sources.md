## Source-selection notes

- The FCA Handbook and legislation.gov.uk are used for current rules/statutory text; FCA, PRA, ICO and UK Government publications for supervisory guidance and context.
- The project does not cite vendor marketing or claim that a named client adopted the prototype pattern.
- All business details, use cases, asset metadata, quality scores, thresholds, governance forums and delivery statuses are project design choices and synthetic data—not facts from these sources.
- Regulatory change is time-sensitive. Recheck consolidated rules, commencement, guidance status, firm scope and regulator publications when beginning a real project.
